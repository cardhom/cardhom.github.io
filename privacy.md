# Cardhom Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Cardhom is an app for keeping your loyalty cards on your phone. It is built so that your data stays on your device: **Cardhom has no server and no accounts**, so we do not receive or store your cards, your locations or any other personal data. The only exceptions are app error reports and the emails you send us, described below.

## Data controller

Davide Saggese, developer of Cardhom. Contact: cardhom.dev@gmail.com

## Data that stays on your phone

This data is stored only in the app's storage on your device. We never receive it and cannot see it.

- **Cards**: barcode, store, notes, badges and the brands you create.
- **Photos of cards**: used to read the code and the store name. Recognition happens on the phone and the app does not keep the photo.
- **Store locations**: the places you link to a store, so the right card shows up when you are nearby.
- **Where you use your cards**, if store learning is on: when you open a card, the app saves the place where you are, with date and time, to work out where that store is and suggest saving it. It does not track your movements at any other time. You can turn it off and clear this data in the settings.
- **App settings**.

The store catalogue ships inside the app: it is not downloaded. The app does not send your location to us or to third parties: it leaves the phone only if you include it in a share or in a backup. Your location is worked out by the system location services (on Android, Google Play services).

The data is kept in the app's private storage, which only Cardhom can access and which the operating system protects; the app does not encrypt it, so we recommend a screen lock (from Android 9 it also encrypts your Google backup). Error reports and ML Kit statistics are sent encrypted (HTTPS).

### Phone backups

If you have turned on device backup (Google backup) or move your data to a new phone, the operating system may include Cardhom's data in your personal backup or in the transfer, including store-learning data (the places where you use your cards). If you clear this data in the settings, the next backup overwrites it. That backup is handled by Google in your account, under their terms: we have no access to it.

## Permissions

- **Camera**: to scan the code on a card or read a sharing QR code. Images are processed on the phone. For photos already on the phone the app uses the system picker, which needs no permission: Cardhom only sees the photos you pick.
- **Location**: to show cards for nearby stores and, if enabled, to learn where the stores you use your cards in are. Your location is used only on the phone and is not sent to us or to third parties.

You can deny or revoke permissions in the system settings: the app keeps working, without the features that need them.

## Sharing cards

You can share cards with someone else through a QR code, shown on screen or sent as an image. The QR code holds the data of the cards you pick (code, store and, if you choose, notes, badges and store locations). Sharing happens directly between people, never through us. If you send the image with another app (a chat, for example), that app handles it under its own privacy policy, and whoever receives it can read that data. Cards you receive from a QR code are saved only on your phone, like the others.

## Error reports (Sentry)

When the app hits an error, it sends a technical report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), a service that helps us find and fix problems.

- **What it contains**: the error type and where in the code it happened, a short technical description written by us (fixed text, with none of your data), the app version, technical information about the device (model, operating system, language, time zone, screen, connection type, available memory, storage and battery) and a random installation ID.
- **What it does not contain**: your card data, notes, locations and the text of error messages, which is removed before sending (only technical codes remain, such as the type of database error). The app sends no screenshots, no activity history and no usage data.
- **IP address**: needed to transmit the report, but Sentry is set up not to store it or use it to work out your location.
- **Who handles them**: Sentry acts as our data processor, under a data processing agreement. Sentry or its providers may access the reports from the United States, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.
- **Where and how long**: individual reports are stored on Sentry servers in the European Union for up to 30 days, then deleted.
- **Legal basis**: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working properly (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **How to turn them off**: in the app settings, with the "Send error reports" switch. From then on the app sends no more reports.

## Google components on the device

Code and text recognition uses Google ML Kit, which runs on the phone: photos are not sent to Google. ML Kit does, however, send Google LLC technical statistics about how it performs (device model, app version, processing times, error codes) and a random installation ID. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in offering recognition that works (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Details: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure

## Emails to support

If you write to cardhom.dev@gmail.com, we use your address and what you tell us only to reply to you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, or our legitimate interest in helping you, Art. 6(1)(f)). The mailbox is on Gmail (Google). We keep messages as long as needed to reply, then for up to 12 months.

## Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, ask for it to be deleted or restricted, and object to its processing by writing to cardhom.dev@gmail.com. Because your card data lives only on your phone, you control and delete it yourself: **uninstalling the app removes it from your phone**; copies in your Google account backup stay until you delete them in your Google settings. Error reports are not linked to your identity, so we may be unable to find the ones about you. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it).

## Children

Cardhom is not intended for children. It does not ask for your age or any other data to know who uses it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
