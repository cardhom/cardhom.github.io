# CardHom Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

CardHom is an app for keeping your loyalty cards on your phone. It is built so that your data stays on your device: **CardHom has no server and no accounts**, so we do not receive or store your cards, your locations or any other personal data. The only exception is app error reports, described below.

## Who we are

Davide Saggese, developer of CardHom. Contact: cardhom.dev@gmail.com

## Data that stays on your phone

This data is stored only in the app's storage on your device. We never receive it and cannot see it.

- **Cards**: barcode, store, notes, badges and the brands you create.
- **Photos of cards**: used to read the code and the store name. Recognition happens on the phone and the app does not keep the photo.
- **Store locations**: the places you link to a store, so the right card shows up when you are nearby.
- **Where you use your cards**, if store learning is on: when you open a card, the app remembers roughly where you are, to work out where that store is and suggest saving it. It does not track your movements at any other time. You can turn it off and clear this data in the settings.
- **App settings**.

The store catalogue ships inside the app: it is not downloaded, and the app never shares your location with anyone.

### Phone backups

If you have turned on device backup (Google backup on Android, iCloud on iPhone), the operating system may include CardHom's data in your personal backup, including store-learning data (the places where you use your cards). If you clear this data in the settings, the next backup overwrites it. That backup is handled by Google or Apple in your account, under their terms: we have no access to it.

## Permissions

- **Camera and photos**: to photograph a card or read a sharing QR code. Images are processed on the phone.
- **Location**: to show cards for nearby stores and, if enabled, to learn where the stores you use your cards in are. Your location is used only on the phone and is never sent anywhere.

You can deny or revoke permissions in the system settings: the app keeps working, without the features that need them.

## Sharing cards

You can share cards with someone else through a QR code, shown on screen or sent as an image. The QR code holds the data of the cards you pick (code, store and, if you choose, notes, badges and store locations). Sharing happens directly between people, never through us. If you send the image with another app (a chat, for example), that app handles it under its own privacy policy, and whoever receives it can read that data.

## Error reports (Sentry)

When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a technical report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), a service that helps us find and fix problems.

- **What it contains**: the error type and where in the code it happened, the app version, technical information about the device (model, operating system, language, time zone, available memory and battery) and a random installation ID.
- **What it does not contain**: your card data, notes, locations and the text of error messages, which is removed before sending (only technical codes remain, such as the type of database error). The app sends no screenshots, no activity history and no usage data.
- **IP address**: needed to transmit the report, but Sentry is set up not to store it or use it to work out your location.
- **Where and how long**: reports are stored on Sentry servers in the European Union for up to 90 days, then deleted.
- **Legal basis**: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working properly (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Google components on the device

Code and text recognition uses Google ML Kit, which runs on the phone: photos are not sent to Google. ML Kit may contact Google servers for updates and to send anonymous technical statistics about how it performs (for example device model and processing times). Details: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure

## Your rights

You have the right to access your data, ask for it to be deleted, or object to its processing by writing to cardhom.dev@gmail.com. Because your card data lives only on your phone, you control and delete it yourself: **uninstalling the app removes all of it** (along with any backups you manage). Error reports are not linked to your identity, so we may be unable to find the ones about you. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it).

## Children

CardHom is not intended for children and does not knowingly collect data from minors.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. For significant changes we will also let you know in the app.
